# Season 1, Episode 1 — The Moon Path

Season: **The Map Pieces.** Ten episodes. Each one finds a piece of a torn map. The finished season is the book.

About 15 minutes if the child listens to each line and answers. Tapping ahead finishes sooner. There is no timer.

Before any episode is built: name the skill, say what changes in the season, put the skill where the story cannot continue without it, list the touches and the words the child can say, write the script, then draw those shots.

## Skill

The sound **mmm**, and the letter **m**. Listening: hear the hum, then choose the path that matches. The child never sees that name.

The hum comes back in the grass, at the gate, on the signs, and on the blank corner of the piece. Same sound, new moment in the walk.

## Script

**CAMP**

LARK: Morning. The map was on this stump.

NIB: I sat on it.

LARK: Nib! You found a piece of the map.

NIB: A moon stone. Where's the rest?

LARK: Torn off. The piece is humming.

LARK: Put your ear close.

LARK: Mmm.

NIB: I can hum too!

LARK: Hold it with me.

LARK: Mmm.

NIB: The sun! Treasure loves the sun!

LARK: He is already running.

**THE FORK**

LARK: Two paths leave camp.

LARK: Nib is on the shiny one.

LARK: The scrap still hums.

LARK: Mmm.

LARK: Which path hums mmm?

*(Sun.)* LARK: That's the sun.

*(Moon. They go.)*

**THE GATE**

LARK: The moon path has a gate.

LARK: Nib shoved it.

LARK: The gate hummed back.

LARK: Same hum as the piece.

LARK: Mmm.

LARK: The gate wants the letter that hums.

*(Wrong stone.)* LARK: Mmm.

**THE PATH**

LARK: Down the path, the grass hums.

LARK: Mmm.

LARK: Your turn. Hum with the piece.

LARK: Mmm.

LARK: Nib found signs.

LARK: He is pointing at the sun one.

LARK: Nib points at the sun.

*(Sun.)* LARK: That's the sun.

**THE WALK**

LARK: You took the moon path.

LARK: The hum came with us.

LARK: Mmm, said the piece.

CHILD: Mmm.

LARK: A moth hummed back.

LARK: The sun did not.

LARK: Nib hummed at a bee.

LARK: Bees do not hum mmm.

LARK: The stone is up there.

CHILD: Mmm.

LARK: The stone.

**THE STONE**

LARK: The stone is glowing in the grass.

LARK: Nib spins around.

LARK: He says the sun led us here.

LARK: Nib says we followed the sun.

*(The sun.)* LARK: Up there.

**THE PIECE**

LARK: The piece has an empty corner.

LARK: Wind tugs at it.

LARK: The letter that hums will hold it.

LARK: Mmm.

LARK: The piece needs its letter.

*(The yellow dot.)* Start here.

**TAG**

LARK: The corner holds.

LARK: First piece of the map. The moon stone too.

LARK: Nib's ears go up. Another hum, far off.

LARK: Moon stone is yours. First piece of the map.

LARK: Nib can see another piece.
