How to play: each word sits on three lily pads, one letter per pad. Gabi slides Pip across with her finger (or taps Pip to watch her slide), and each pad says its sound as Pip passes. Then pictures float up in bubbles and she taps the word she read. Five words make a splash party with an off-screen idea. Every word she reads goes in Pip's treasure chest, and a gold star means three right in a row.

How it adapts: it starts with short-a words that begin with stretchy sounds (fan, map, van), then adds a word each time she knows nearly all of the current ones, moving through short a, o, i, u, then e. Right answers make the wrong pictures trickier (from "fan or dog" up to "cat, cap or bat", where she has to blend every sound). A miss makes them easier again, replays the sounds, and after two misses the right bubble glows. Once she's read a word right twice in a row, the pads go quiet on that word so she says the sounds herself (she can still tap a pad to hear it).

Every answer is sent to Joyce as "Blending CVC words" for the weekly report. Hold the gear button to open this panel.
