The Ramp by the Cart
Lark & Nib's Treasure Quest · Season 4, Episode 3

The small book cart caught at the ramp's raised end. Nib pushed once and made it more firmly stuck. Lark noticed the unused lower support slot at the landing. Sea waited safely in the cart; the other three books remained aboard.

“A cart is excellent at going,” Nib grumbled mildly. “A ramp helps with where,” Lark observed.

Lark opened an earlier journal page on a flat, dry surface. Nib steadied its lower corner. One familiar word waited for another look.

“Try our earlier word before we start,” Lark invited gently. “You can sound it out or ask for help.”

> chip

“Ar gives the vowel sound in cart,” Lark modeled clearly. “Hear it as a joined part rather than a short a followed by a new syllable. Read cart, farm, and park with the pronunciation we use in speech.”

“Join the parts and check the whole meaning,” Lark invited gently. “You can hear a part again whenever you want.”

On a little practice strip, you could try cart, farm, park. Lark supplied unfamiliar story names herself. Nib waited while you looked right through each word.

“Read cart on the load diagram,” Lark invited gently. “Which slot puts its ramp against a real support?”

A short passage stayed in view. Lark paired each needed capital with its lowercase form and familiar sound before you began, leaving the sentence meanings for you to discover.

“Read the sentences in order,” Lark invited gently. “Let your finger follow if it helps. When you are ready, use the words to answer our question.”

> A cart is on a ramp. Sam can park it.

“What can Sam park?” Lark asked gently. “Use the first sentence to explain it.”

Lark kept the passage open so you could point to the part that helped. Nib listened before adding the picture.

Nib backed the cart onto the level deck. Together they lowered the ramp into the proper slot and tested its seating. Lark steadied the side while Nib rolled the cart ashore. The wheels crossed the join smoothly. The Commons borrower waited near a supported shelf with two fern-marked papers beside it.

“Let's keep a written clue,” Lark suggested warmly. “Write cart beside our picture. You can look at the example, follow it first, or try your own version. Then reread what you made.”

Your journal showed the supported ramp join. Sea had reached Commons, but the loan still needed the correct borrower and record. Arriving at a shelf was not the same as finishing the handoff.
