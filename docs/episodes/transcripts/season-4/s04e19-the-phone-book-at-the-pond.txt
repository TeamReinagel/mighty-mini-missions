Lark & Nib's Treasure Quest · Season 4, Episode 19

“A phone with paper pages,” Nib murmured, delighted. “A story about messages,” Lark explained warmly.

“Join the parts and check the whole meaning,” Lark invited gently. “You can hear a part again whenever you want.”
