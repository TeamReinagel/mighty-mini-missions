Lark & Nib's Treasure Quest · Season 6, Episode 2

“A new workshop would solve it,” Nib suggested hopefully. “Refitting one tray might be quicker,” Lark replied gently.

“Join the parts and check the whole meaning,” Lark invited gently. “You can hear a part again whenever you want.”
