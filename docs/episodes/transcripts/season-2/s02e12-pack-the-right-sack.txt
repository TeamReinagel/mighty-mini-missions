Lark & Nib's Treasure Quest · Season 2, Episode 12

At Fern Lodge, Nib wrapped the final invitation in spare cloth. Then he added another piece, and another, until the destination mark vanished. The caretaker's receiving instructions were fixed beside a folded sack. Lark looked from the neat instruction to the enormous anonymous bundle.

“Packed beyond all recognition,” Nib reported proudly. “Recognition is part of the job,” Lark reminded him kindly.

Lark opened an earlier journal page on a flat, dry surface. Nib steadied its lower corner. One familiar word waited for another look.

“Try our earlier word before we start,” Lark invited gently. “You can sound it out or ask for help.”
