Lark & Nib's Treasure Quest · Season 5, Episode 10

“It is all turning, except the bit that is not,” Nib reported carefully. “That difference matters,” Lark replied.

“Join the parts and check the whole meaning,” Lark invited gently. “You can hear a part again whenever you want.”

“Read the ongoing and completed descriptions,” Lark invited gently. “Which one matches the wheel now, and which matches yesterday's screen work?”

Your journal showed two simultaneous states. Nib traced the linkage on paper rather than poking at the mechanism. The next test would use the lever that actually did the reset job.
