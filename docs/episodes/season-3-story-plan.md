## The starting state

The library and woodland post route are working. Lookout Hill’s reply asks the crew to help read and restore an old signal lantern before its next community lighting. The original map remains complete at camp; a borrowed hill guide, protected kit, and courier bag travel with Lark, Nib, and the child.

## Plan the ending first

The hill lantern shines through its properly aligned shutter and window, showing a clear ordinary light toward the woodland meeting place. The crew has reached the lookout, diagnosed the scattered faults, and followed the final short instructions without inventing a new moonstone power. Neighbors see the agreed signal. A brass star badge and a small viewing lens commemorate the work; The Long Path becomes book three.

## The mini novel

The Lookout Hill guide promises a view beyond the woods, but its short labels use spellings the crew has not read before. Their climb alternates travel decisions with preparations for the lantern: a reliable container, transport for the glass, a fitted cover, and a stable stopping place. At the lookout they discover that the lamp itself is not the only problem. A misplaced window, rattling connector, and stuck shade prevent the light from reaching its intended direction. Lark initially treats every fault as a separate reason to start over. Nib wants the lamp bright before the rest of it is ready. By testing one part at a time they bring the complete assembly into order. Their final act is not finding another secret cache, but making a public signal that others can use. The long path has widened their view and their responsibility.

## Work backward from that ending

1. The final beam needs an aligned window, secured ring, usable shutter, and a clearly identified target direction; establish each in the hill chapters.
2. The hill work needs glass and fittings to arrive intact; the van, case, and padded box chapters make that possible.
3. The climb needs correct route choices from the borrowed guide; teach its new spellings before requiring those labels.
4. The signal is an agreed community light, not a star or a distress signal; establish its purpose in the opening invitation.

## Character movement

Lark learns to test one useful hypothesis rather than demand an immediately perfect system. Nib learns to prepare an action before announcing its success. The child applies a familiar blending method to new spellings, so longer-looking labels do not become a reason to guess from the picture.

## Object, information, and destination ledger

- The hill guide is borrowed in 1 and returned after 14; the original map is not torn or transported unnecessarily.
- Jar/container, delivery van, glass case, box, ring, and shade each have one physical use and remain accounted for until installed or packed away.
- The caretaker supplies and lights the ordinary lantern; the crew adjusts supported parts. Moonstone still only reveals hidden ink and is not used as a battery.
- The final light aims at the previously agreed woodland meeting place; it does not promise navigation accuracy, rescue services, or actual astronomical observations.

## Earlier setups and later payoffs

- The distant tree cue in 1 leads to the actual hill route, not an unrelated later forest.
- The covered glass from 4 is installed in 8–9.
- The line established in 10 is the alignment reference at the finale.
- Nib’s repeated urge to hurry in 5 is paid off when he waits for the final lift/shut check.

## Teaching and review handoff

Every chapter carries the entire earlier taught set forward. Its listed review is a selection, not a reset. New sound/spelling models precede child use. Words can be read as soon as their mappings are introduced; no later “blending” chapter postpones that work. The cumulative ledger in the series roadmap records the exact introduction points for patterns and explicit function words.

The example lists are deliberately limited child-reading targets. A character name, chapter title, spoken sound-bank word, or narrated instruction is not automatically decodable. Narrator-supplied names and untaught text stay supplied. Capital forms are paired explicitly with familiar lowercase forms before they are required. Actual responses guide support; completion is not mastery.

For each new-code chapter, model the focus and use its sound bank disproportionately in natural prose. For comprehension chapters, state the text strategy and continue selective decoding review. Use expressive speech attributions frequently; audition the intended delivery in the recording. Check the entire drafted child-readable passage against the ledger, not only these examples.

## Physical staging and production

Reuse the woodland trail and ridge with a new broad hill landing and lookout platform. The van is a small woodland delivery vehicle, sized for the established characters. Glass, rings, shutters, and rails attach to actual frames; Lark’s flight scouts a view but cannot transport all the heavy fittings.

Before generating a plate, specify foot/prop contact planes, attachment points, walking corridors, and moving-platform parents. Composite the real layers at tablet crops. Mouth-only speech and body-only walking remain the motion style. Plan scenes around actual supporting geometry; do not reuse an attractive river plate by placing Nib on its water.

Choose a story-audio budget from the total target after reserving ordinary response and transition time. Measure an expressive read-through, then the recording and played chapter. Never insert extra practice or hurry speech just to meet a duration. New audio requires its own alignment and cuts. Scripts, art, recordings, and builds remain separate production work.

## Resolution and next-season invitation

The lantern is working, its purpose fulfilled, and book three is bound. A visitor who saw the light brings a Storyboat invitation: a new riverside reading route needs a crew. The lantern remains available to the community rather than becoming a portable superpower.

## Writing acceptance

Before a script is recorded, verify that each opening follows the previous end state, each chapter resolves its own question, and each destination follows from something actually observed or read. Check the ledger, review opportunities, independent/support distinctions, and setup/payoff chain. No unprepared word, new magical power, or hidden assessment threshold may appear only at the finale.
