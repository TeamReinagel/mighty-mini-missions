## Required passage check before recording

1. Mark every span the child is expected to read, including menu labels if those labels are necessary to act. Titles and rich narration may remain adult-read.
2. Check the actual pronunciation and spelling of each required word against the introduced mappings, not only whether its letters have appeared.
3. Supply or explicitly model untaught topic vocabulary; do not report the supplied answer as independent reading.
4. Check capital forms, function words, suffix pronunciations, and base-spelling changes.
5. For a fresh reading page, do not read its answer aloud first or reveal it through a glowing picture.
6. Let the response change support, feedback, wording, or the next action; never use a complete episode as a substitute for evidence of a particular skill.

The structural check for this packet verifies ordering of its explicitly annotated features and basic letters. Manual pronunciation review remains necessary: the same letters can represent different sounds, and no letter-only check proves decodability. Quiet, route, word/work, field, and other unmodeled alternative spellings remain supplied rather than silently becoming targets.

## Assessment and support boundaries

Introduced is different from learned. R review, M modeled practice, A application, and S story action are distinguished in the briefs. A tap does not establish oral decoding; an echoed model is supported. Fresh-page help stays available, and no accuracy gate blocks the novel’s ending. Actual first responses guide future review.
