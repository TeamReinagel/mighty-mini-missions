How to play

  1. 1

    Listen for the sight word.

  2. 2

    Drag Boo left or right to line up.

  3. 3

    Fly through the matching word to collect the sweet candy!

Keep flying and collecting candies as the trail gets faster and faster! You have 3 chances. If you miss a word, look out for small glowing winged hearts floating between gates to restore a missed chance! Finish a run within 90% of your best and Boo gets to go trick-or-treating: knock on the door whose word rhymes for candy, and find one new costume each visit. Each costume has a special power, and different powers help on different trails, so choose wisely to reach your goal!

For the most natural voice, download "Ava (Premium)" or "Zoe (Premium)", then reopen the game. iPad: Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > English. Mac: System Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > System Voice > Manage Voices.
