How to play: each Mama bird wears a big letter. Tap the egg with the matching little letter (or drag it into the nest) and it hatches. Five babies fill a nest, then the round ends with a celebration and an off-screen idea. Every hatched letter gets a nest in the Baby Tree; a gold star means three right in a row.

How it adapts: it starts with letters that look the same big and little (C c, O o, S s...), adds a new letter each time she knows nearly all of the current ones, and saves look-alikes (b d p q, m n) for later. Right answers add eggs (up to 4); a miss drops back to fewer. After two misses the right egg glows, so every round ends in a hatch.

Every answer is sent to Joyce as "Big & little letters" for the weekly report. Hold the gear button to open this panel.
