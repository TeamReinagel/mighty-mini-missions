The Send to Joyce button

  1. Add this button to your bookmarks: drag it to the bookmarks bar, or on a phone follow the steps below.
  2. Each week, open AdaptedMind's recent lessons, signed in as usual.
  3. When the page has loaded, tap the bookmark. This page opens and sends it to Joyce.
Send to Joyce
