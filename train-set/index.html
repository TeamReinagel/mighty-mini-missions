How to play: Track: drag a finger to lay track (pieces connect themselves), tap a shape to add a station, tap the magic wand for a ready-made track, use the sponge to erase. Train: tap a cargo car to add it, tap a car on the train to send it away, tap the engine to change its color. Play: put a finger on the train and drag it along the track; at a junction it takes the branch your finger heads toward. Holding a finger anywhere near the track calls the train to it. Or press the green button to let it drive. Tap stations, the train, or the star for a "find it" game.

iPad tips: Share, then Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app. Turn on Guided Access (Settings, Accessibility) and triple-click the side button to lock Ethan into the game. Hold the gear button to open this panel; hold the trash can to clear the track.
